Trading from live prices

Your own copy of the galaxy's markets, updated the moment any commander docks.

Where the prices come from

Elite Nexus listens to the EDDN feed, where commanders' tools share market data as they dock, and keeps it in a local database together with your own journal. An optional one-time import of Spansh's public daily dump means every station is known from day one. Because the database is on your PC, the best price within 50 ly comes back in about a millisecond, and trade routes in a few dozen.

What you can ask it

  • Sell my cargo: the best single stop for your whole hold, re-planned after every jump.
  • Abnormal prices: stations and fleet carriers paying far more than a commodity's galactic average, or selling far below it. Pick the multiple (1.5× to 10×), the area and how old prices may be.
  • Find a commodity: best price or nearest, with pad size, stock and age.
  • Trade routes for your ship and credits.
  • Price alerts: "tell me when Tritium sells for 60,000+ within 50 ly", spoken seconds after a commander docks there.
  • Nearest services: material traders, technology brokers, Interstellar Factors, Vista Genomics, and mining hotspots with the best sell price.
  • Colonisation: where to buy each commodity a construction depot still needs.
Good to know. Prices are only as fresh as the last commander who docked there with a data-sharing tool, so results show their age. Sharing your own market visits with EDDN is opt-in, and you can pause it while docked to keep a market you just found to yourself.
