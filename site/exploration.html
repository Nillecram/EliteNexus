Exploration & navigation

Know what's worth the detour.

See the value in the system around you, choose the next worlds to scan and follow the journey without losing track of your discoveries. Exploration, travel planning and your galaxy map work together.

Read the system at a glance

Decide what deserves a closer look.

As you scan, Elite Nexus builds a tree of stars, planets and moons. Current scan value and potential mapped value sit side by side, with valuable discoveries highlighted.

The next decision is in the detail

Open a body to inspect materials, rings and hotspots, biological signals, and discovery and footfall information from your journal. Earth-like worlds, water worlds, ammonia worlds and terraformables are easy to pick out.

Choose whether a long flight to a planet is worth the additional mapping value before you leave the arrival star. The Codex view keeps your recorded discoveries within reach, too.

Hear the discovery as it happens

Optional spoken alerts announce valuable bodies and biological signals while you fly. Unsurveyed and populated systems are explained in the page, so missing scans are not presented as a system worth zero.

Find valuable bodies

Make a scanning trip worth taking.

Plan a tour of Earth-likes, water and ammonia worlds, and terraformables with the built-in Spansh Road to Riches planner.

  1. Choose how you want to explore

    Rank by the FSS scan alone or the value after DSS mapping. Set the minimum value, arrival distance and search area to suit the time you want to spend.

  2. Review the worlds on your route

    See the systems, target bodies and estimated values before committing. The planner supports searches up to 1,000 ly and 2,000 stops.

  3. Follow it in Navigation

    Send the tour to Navigation, where stops advance as you arrive and the next system can be copied to your clipboard automatically.

Starting in the bubble?

Spansh excludes populated systems from these tours. Elite Nexus widens an empty search in stages, up to 1,000 ly, and tells you when it has done so.

Navigation

One place to follow every journey.

Your in-game route, Spansh travel plans and exploration or exobiology tours come together in Navigation. See star classes, scoopable stops, neutron and white-dwarf hazards, and the longest stretch without a scoopable star.

Neutron routes

Plan travel through neutron stars using your jump range, with routing efficiency available in the options.

Exact ship routes

Plan each jump using your ship's FSD, mass, fuel and cargo. Adjust the routing strategy, neutron boosts, injections and refuelling choices.

Fleet carrier routes

Plan carrier travel with its tritium and cargo in view, then review the jumps, route distance and tritium requirement.

Your galaxy map

See how far you've come. Choose where to go.

Explore the Milky Way with your visited systems, travel path, active route, fleet carrier, parked ships and landmarks on the same map.

A map you can use

Pan and zoom, switch layers, fit all your travels into view or return to your current position. Search a visited system or a landmark such as Colonia, Sagittarius A* or Beagle Point.

Select a system to see its distance, copy its name or open Navigation with it as your destination.

Bring the discoveries home

Know what is still waiting to be sold.

Unsold data totals your cartographic discoveries, with current and mapped values by system. History keeps the wider record: what you scanned, what sold and when, and what happened to the rest.

A history that explains the totals

Filter systems by sold, unsold, not sellable or lost data. Scans Universal Cartographics left out of a sale and data lost with your ship are identified separately, so they do not quietly inflate your unsold total.

Keep following the interesting signals

Biological signals can turn a scanning trip into a surface expedition. Move to Exobiology for species predictions, sampling distance guidance and routes to promising planets.
