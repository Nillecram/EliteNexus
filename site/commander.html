Engineering

From the ship you have to the build you want.

Start with a specific blueprint or add an upgrade to Grade 5 from your current ship. Elite Nexus collects the material requirements in one place.

  1. Build the wishlist

    Add the blueprints you want, or use the ship's quick action to plan an upgrade to G5.

  2. See the shopping list

    Combine the requirements across your plans and check them against your material inventory.

  3. Plan your trades

    The material trader plan shows what to exchange and which kind of trader you need.

Find the right engineer

Engineers appear unlocked first, then by distance, so your available options are easy to find.

Keep the rest of your kit together

The engineering pages also cover Odyssey suits and weapons, synthesis, materials and your ship locker.

Ships & fleet carrier

Know what you own. Know where it is.

Your fleet view brings together parked ships, transfers, loadouts and engineering, while the carrier page follows the larger operation.

Inspect the exact build

Open a ship to see its modules, engineering and modifier changes. Send that loadout to EDSY or Coriolis when you want to work on a build there.

Follow your carriers

Track tritium, finances, capacity, crew, services and orders for your own fleet carrier and your squadron carrier. The jump schedule, countdown and history keep their movements visible, and a decommission shows its scrap date.

These views follow the state reported by the game. Carrier controls and ship changes remain in Elite Dangerous.

Missions & influence

See the work still ahead.

Keep mission progress and faction activity alongside the ship you are flying.

Track the mission stack

Follow active missions, expiry times, cargo deliveries and kill progress. The massacre stack view accounts for kills once per issuing faction, showing the remaining kills across the stack.

Review BGS & Powerplay activity

See what you did for each faction, including a view since the last tick. Set the tick hour used by your group; fleet carrier transactions are excluded from BGS activity.

Colonisation

Turn a depot list into the next cargo run.

Construction depots show the tonnage still required for every commodity and how much is already in your hold.

  1. Check what remains

    See outstanding requirements at the construction site, commodity by commodity.

  2. Use the cargo you carry

    Compare the requirements with your current hold before planning another purchase.

  3. Find the next supplier

    Use Where to buy for nearby stations with enough recorded stock for a full hold, measured from the construction site. Check the market record before you travel.

Controls

Keep the right action within reach.

Inspect your active control presets and turn a long bindings list into a reference you can use.

Find an action or a conflict

Search your bindings, see the active presets for each category, and review conflicts and missing essentials. Elite Nexus only reads your bindings; it never changes them.

Keep a reference beside you

Create a printable reference card for your controls. It opens as an HTML page, ready to print or keep nearby.
