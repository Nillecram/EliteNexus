How Elite Nexus compares

Many commanders run four to eight tools at once. Elite Nexus puts the everyday jobs in one app. Here's what it covers, and where the long-standing tools are still the better choice.

These tools are made by volunteers who built the Elite community's tooling over a decade, and Elite Nexus relies on their work (EDDN, Spansh, EDSM, EDCD's data). Use what serves you best; nothing stops you running Elite Nexus next to any of them except where noted.

Why one app

  • One data layer. Your journal, the EDDN market feed and Spansh feed every page at once, so a route planned in Exobiology shows up in Navigation, and the assistant can answer from all of it.
  • Speed. Native Rust with a GPU-drawn interface. Your history loads in well under a second, and local market searches take milliseconds.
  • One download. No Python, Java or .NET runtime and no installer: unzip and run.

What Elite Nexus doesn't do (yet)

  • No plugins, no in-game overlay, no VR panels, no voice commands.
  • Windows only.
  • No Frontier Companion API yet; the journal already provides almost everything.
  • It's a young app by a single developer. Bugs and requests go to GitHub issues.
