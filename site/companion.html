AI co-pilot

Ask from where you are.

The Ask assistant can work from your location, ship, cargo, credits and journal. It uses tools to look up game data and a built-in knowledge base for mechanics and app guidance.

Find a useful next job

Ask where to sell your cargo, look for price deals, compare trade routes, or find mining hotspots and nearby services. Results depend on the data available to the app.

Look beyond the next jump

Query systems and planets, investigate biology, or plan an exploration or exobiology trip through Spansh. Record age and coverage matter, especially for first footfall and bonus hunting.

Turn an answer into a route

Routes planned by the assistant appear on Navigation, where you can follow the stops as you travel. Journal search can also help you find something from an earlier session.

Useful questions to start with

“Where should I sell my cargo?” · “Plan an exobiology trip near me.” · “Where can I make money with this ship?”

The assistant can make mistakes. Check the destination, data age and route details before committing to a trip.

Choose your model

Connect the provider that suits you.

Gemini is the default setup. Open Other providers and model to choose Claude, a local model or a compatible custom service.

Google Gemini

Use the setup link to obtain your own API key, paste it and connect. Model availability, limits, pricing and data terms are set by Google.

Anthropic Claude

Choose Claude and provide your own API key. Requests use your Anthropic account and its applicable pricing and data terms.

A model on your PC

Run a tool-capable model in Ollama or LM Studio, then choose Local model and its address. The model server must be running before Ask can answer.

What a model receives

With a cloud provider, your questions, conversation, commander context and tool results used for the answer are sent to that provider. A server running locally keeps model inference on your PC; tools that query Spansh or other online services still make network requests. API keys are stored encrypted with Windows DPAPI (on Linux and macOS, unencrypted in a settings file only your user account can read).

Dashboard & compact HUD

Keep the flight essentials visible.

The dashboard gathers commander ranks, credits, location, ship status, holdings and current-system values, with a session view of your activity.

Follow the session

See earnings and activity since you logged in. Transfers to your own carrier are kept out of the session's spending total.

Make room for the game

Switch to a small always-on-top panel for a second monitor or the edge of your screen. It shows location, fuel, the next star and whether it is scoopable, sampling radar, system value and recent alerts.

Use Full view to return to the complete app.

Voice & flight history

Hear what matters. Find what happened.

Alerts and searchable records help you follow a busy session and return to the details later.

Choose your spoken alerts

Enable voice (Windows speech, speech-dispatcher on Linux, or the system voice on macOS) and set the minimum alert priority. Events include critical hull, valuable bodies, biology samples, motherlodes, low fuel before a non-scoopable star and scheduled carrier jumps.

Search the logbook

Find journal events by system, station or commander name. Review travel, docking, the credit ledger and the original event details.

Read your statistics

Follow credits over time, income and spending by category, jumps per day and the statistics reported by the game.

Data & sync

Choose what you share.

Elite Nexus reads your game journals and keeps its settings, database and logs locally. Community uploads and AI provider requests have separate purposes and controls.

Enable community uploads individually

EDDN, EDSM, Inara and EDAstro sharing are opt-in. These integrations send live events rather than uploading your journal history, with persistent queues and service rate limits.

See when a service needs attention

Accounts & sync shows sent, queued and failed counts, plus reasons for a pause. Turning a service off discards its pending queue. Keys are encrypted on your Windows account (on Linux and macOS, readable only by your user account).

What does Inara need before it can sync?

Inara requires approval for an app's name as well as a valid user API key. Until Elite Nexus is approved, Inara rejects requests and the app pauses sync with an explanation.

Does disabling community sync also make cloud AI private?

Ask sends the information needed for its responses to the model provider you select. The Accounts & sync switches control community uploads; they do not prevent those AI requests. Choose a local model server if you want model inference on your PC.

Can I use the other pages without setting up AI?

Yes. Ask is optional. The dashboard, journal tools and other feature pages work independently of an AI provider; online features still require their respective services.
