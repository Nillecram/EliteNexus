A theme worth keeping

Build it once. Keep it. Share it.

  1. Save your favourites

    Name a theme and choose Save theme. Your custom palettes join the presets, ready to revisit whenever you want a different cockpit.

  2. Bring the community with you

    Use Copy as XML to share your three colour-matrix lines. Found a theme on a forum? Paste those lines into Use a theme someone shared to load it into the editor.

  3. Apply, then launch

    Choose Apply to Elite Dangerous. Nexus updates only the colour entries in your graphics override and keeps your original file. Quit the game to the desktop and restart to see your new colours.

Know your controls

The right adjustment for each part of the cockpit.

Does the preview show exactly how the game will look?

It is an interactive illustration of the colour changes. The game adds glow, transparency and its own rendering, so the final appearance can differ. Check your theme in the game after restarting.

Can I give each HUD element its own colour?

The game’s colour matrix affects the interface together, including station menus, the on-foot HUD and mission-board portraits. Elite Nexus can preserve the standard contact and highlight colours with its graphics hook, but it does not assign a separate custom colour to every element.

Do ordinary HUD themes need the graphics hook?

No. Presets, custom themes and brightness adjustments use the game’s own settings file. The optional hook is needed to preserve contact and highlight colours. It cannot run alongside EDHM or another 3Dmigoto mod; Nexus detects those installations. EDHM also expects the game’s matrix to remain standard orange.

What about bright cockpit lamps and glare?

HUD brightness, cockpit lighting and the way the scene is exposed are separate controls. Use Cockpit lights to dim the cabin lamps, and Glare and exposure to tune glare and dark tones.
