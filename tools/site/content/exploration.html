Find valuable bodies

Make a scanning trip worth taking.

Plan a tour of Earth-likes, water and ammonia worlds, and terraformables with the built-in Spansh Road to Riches planner.

  1. Choose how you want to explore

    Rank by the FSS scan alone or the value after DSS mapping. Set the minimum value, arrival distance and search area to suit the time you want to spend.

  2. Review the worlds on your route

    See the systems, target bodies and estimated values before committing. The planner supports searches up to 1,000 ly and 2,000 stops.

  3. Follow it in Navigation

    Send the tour to Navigation, where stops advance as you arrive and the next system can be copied to your clipboard automatically.

Starting in the bubble?

Spansh excludes populated systems from these tours. Elite Nexus widens an empty search in stages, up to 1,000 ly, and tells you when it has done so.

Bring the discoveries home

Know what is still waiting to be sold.

Unsold data totals your cartographic discoveries, with current and mapped values by system. History keeps the wider record: what you scanned, what sold and when, and what happened to the rest.

A history that explains the totals

Filter systems by sold, unsold, not sellable or lost data. Scans Universal Cartographics left out of a sale and data lost with your ship are identified separately, so they do not quietly inflate your unsold total.
