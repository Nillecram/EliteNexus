Why it is here: this is the most current permissively licensed engineering dataset
(on-foot costs after the 2022 rebalance incl. credits, Colonia on-foot engineers,
engineer locations/requirements, tech-broker list incl. SCO FSDs and AX items).
Ingredient strings use EDD short codes ("5MRCOL" = 5 x Optical Lens) that are
resolved through MCMRType.cs. Known data bugs: mine-launcher blueprints are tagged
ModuleTypes.LifeSupport; several Colonia engineers' max grades are 4 where the game
offers 5 (Etienne Dorn, Petra Olmanova, Marsha Hicks); Dazzle Shell lists Mechanical
Scrap twice. See docs/research/engineering.md.
