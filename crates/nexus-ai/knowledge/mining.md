# Mining

## Methods
- Laser mining: shoot asteroids with mining lasers, collect the fragments with collector limpets, refine them in the refinery. Works for Platinum, Painite, Low Temperature Diamonds, Osmium, Tritium and most metals.
- Core (deep core) mining: find motherlode asteroids with the pulse wave analyser, place seismic charges in the fissures, detonate, collect the chunks. Yields Void Opals, Low Temperature Diamonds, Alexandrite, Grandidierite, Monazite, Musgravite, Rhodplumsite, Serendibite, Benitoite and Bromellite.
- Sub-surface deposits: blast surface deposits with the sub-surface displacement missile, then the abrasion blaster.

## Hotspots and rings
A detailed surface scan of a planetary ring shows hotspots: concentrations of one commodity. Overlapping hotspots (two or more of the same commodity) give much faster yields. Metallic rings: Platinum, Painite, Palladium, Gold, Osmium. Icy rings: Low Temperature Diamonds, Void Opals, Tritium, Bromellite. Rocky and metal-rich rings: Alexandrite, Monazite, Grandidierite, Serendibite, Benitoite and others. Elite Nexus' Market > Mining hotspots tab finds known hotspots near the commander together with the best places to sell.

## Prospecting and efficiency
Fire a prospector limpet at an asteroid first: it shows the material content (and "motherlode" for core asteroids). Only mine asteroids with a high percentage of the target material. Fit enough collector limpet controllers, a large refinery and plenty of cargo racks. Elite Nexus tracks tonnes per hour and motherlodes on the Mining page.

## Selling mined goods
Mining income depends almost entirely on where it is sold: prices differ by millions of credits per hold between stations and change daily. Use "Sell my cargo" or the best-sell list before leaving the ring. Large amounts can exceed one station's demand; split the sale or pick a station with high demand.

## Good mining ships
Large: Type-9, Type-10, Imperial Cutter, Anaconda, Panther Clipper Mk II. Medium: Python, Krait Mk II, Type-8. Small starter: Cobra Mk III or Asp Explorer with a couple of mining lasers. Bring a fuel scoop or a carrier nearby.
