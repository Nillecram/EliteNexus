# Combat and missions

## Bounty hunting
Wanted ships in resource extraction sites (RES), navigation beacons and compromised beacons pay bounties, collected at Interstellar Factors or at stations of the issuing faction's jurisdiction. Kill warrant scanners add bounties from other jurisdictions. Hazardous RES pay more but are more dangerous.

## Massacre mission stacking
Several factions in nearby systems often offer missions to kill pirates of the same target faction. Each kill counts for one mission from every issuing faction at once, so accepting missions from many different factions (up to 20 missions in total) multiplies the payout. Elite Nexus' Missions page shows the real number of kills still needed across the stack.

## Conflict zones
War and civil-war states create conflict zones (in space and, in Odyssey, on foot at settlements). Combat bonds are paid by the faction the commander fights for, redeemed at its stations.

## Staying safe
- Keep the rebuy cost in credits.
- Interdictions: submitting (throttle to zero) gives a shorter FSD cooldown than losing the minigame; boost away and jump if outmatched.
- Crimes give fines and bounties; notoriety rises with murders and blocks paying off bounties for a while.
- Heat, shields and module health: check after neutron boosts and combat.

## Thargoid and Guardian content
Fighting Thargoids needs anti-xeno (AX) weapons and modules, many of them Guardian technology unlocked from Guardian sites. The state of the Thargoid conflict changes with game updates; check Canonn Research and the Frontier forums for the current situation.
