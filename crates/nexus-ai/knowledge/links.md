# Websites and tools

## Community websites
- Inara (inara.cz): commander profiles, market and commodity search, engineers and blueprints, galaxy and faction information, community goals, squadrons.
- Spansh (spansh.co.uk): the best route planners (neutron, exact plotter, fleet carrier, road to riches, exobiology, trade, colonisation) and fast search over systems, bodies and stations. Elite Nexus uses Spansh's planners directly.
- EDSM (edsm.net): flight logs, a 3D star map and system data contributed by commanders.
- EDSY (edsy.org) and Coriolis (coriolis.io): ship builders to plan and compare loadouts; Elite Nexus opens the current ship in either with one click (Fleet page).
- EDAstro (edastro.com): exploration maps and statistics of the whole galaxy.
- Canonn Research (canonn.science): research into Guardians, Thargoids, codex entries and biology.
- Elite Dangerous Wiki (elite-dangerous.fandom.com): reference for ships, modules, mechanics and lore.
- Frontier forums (forums.frontier.co.uk): official patch notes and announcements.
- EDRefCard (edrefcard.info): printable key-binding reference cards (Elite Nexus has its own on the Bindings page).
- ED Tools (edtools.cc): community helpers such as a massacre-mission finder and mining tools.
- The Fuel Rats (fuelrats.com): free rescue when a ship runs out of fuel.

## The data network behind it all
EDDN (Elite Dangerous Data Network) relays market, outfitting, shipyard and exploration data that commanders' tools upload, in real time. Inara, Spansh and EDSM all build their databases from it. Elite Nexus listens to EDDN directly, so its market data is as fresh as anyone's, and can contribute back (opt-in, with a Pause button).

## Tools Elite Nexus replaces
EDDiscovery, ED Market Connector, Odyssey Materials Helper, Observatory and its BioInsights plugin, Exploration Buddy, cockpit voice assistants such as EDCoPilot, and Elite tracker overlays: journal history, exploration values, exobiology sampling and predictions, market search, engineering planning, uploads to EDDN, EDSM, Inara and EDAstro, and spoken alerts are all built in.
