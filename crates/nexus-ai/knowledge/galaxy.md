# Colonisation, Powerplay and the BGS

## Colonisation
Commanders can claim an uninhabited system near existing populated space through the System Colonisation contact, then build stations, outposts, settlements and installations there. Building works by hauling commodities to a construction site; each site lists what it still needs (steel, aluminium, CMM composite, insulating membranes, liquid oxygen and many more) and pays per tonne delivered. Elite Nexus' Colonisation page shows every construction site the commander docked at, what is still needed, what is already in the hold, and "Where to buy": the nearest stations with enough stock for a full hold of each commodity.

## The background simulation (BGS)
Every populated system has minor factions with an influence share. Missions, trade, bounties, combat bonds and exploration data sold at a faction's stations raise its influence; crimes against it lower it. Influence changes are applied once a day at the "tick". Factions enter states such as Boom, Expansion, War, Election, Civil Unrest or Outbreak that change markets and missions. Elite Nexus' BGS page lists what the commander did for which faction since the last tick.

## Powerplay
Commanders can pledge to one of the galactic powers and earn merits by activities in that power's systems (trading, combat, exploration, data delivery and more). Merits raise the commander's powerplay rank, which unlocks rewards such as special modules, and help the power reinforce or acquire systems. Powerplay rules changed substantially with Powerplay 2.0; check the in-game Powerplay screen for the current activities.
